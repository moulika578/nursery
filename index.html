Rooted in soil, growing since 2006

Sree Nursery began as a small family plot with a hundred saplings. Today our four-acre nursery raises over 1200 varieties — every one hardened in open beds before it is sold, so your plant survives the move home.

We pot in our own compost blend, ship in shock-proof packaging and stay on call for aftercare. No plastic fillers, no forced-grown stock.
