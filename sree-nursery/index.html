— Our House

A small studio on purpose.

Sree began in 2019 as a single room above a tailor's shop in Jayanagar, Bangalore. The founder, Sree Lakshmi, had spent eight years between Chennai and Mumbai apprenticing under couturiers who insisted that fabric should obey the body, not the other way around.

We have grown — carefully — into a three-karigar atelier. We turn down more commissions than we accept, because each piece is signed by the person who made it, and a karigar can only sign what they have actually stitched.

"If I cannot tell you which hands made your dress, I should not be selling it to you." — Sree Lakshmi, Founder
